# EDA summary

Generated by `python -m src.eda` from `data/processed/model_table.csv` (1,180 listings). Prices are 2024 asking prices, not sale prices.

## Five findings

1. **Land in the city costs the most.** A square meter of land (with its house) in Kota Yogyakarta is listed at a median of Rp 9,8 jt, about 1.5× Bantul (Rp 6,5 jt) and 2.5× Kulon Progo (Rp 3,9 jt).
2. **Prices fall with distance from the center.** Within 5 km of Tugu the median is Rp 10 jt per m² of land; more than 15 km away it is Rp 3,9 jt — about 2.6× less.
3. **Price grows roughly in step with land size.** Across all listings, a house on twice the land is listed for about 2.0× the price. Land size is the strongest single clue to price.
4. **Listing keywords mostly describe the type of house, not extra value.** Listings mentioning an SHM certificate have a lower median price (Rp 696 jt vs Rp 1,1 M) because they are smaller houses (70 vs 100 m² of building). Listings with a pool are 2.7× pricier, but also much bigger.
5. **The data is concentrated in a few places.** 72% of listings are in Sleman, and three kecamatan (Ngaglik, Depok, Kalasan) hold 45% of them. 22 of the 43 kecamatan with data have fewer than 10 listings, and Gunungkidul has only 6 — estimates there will be rough.
